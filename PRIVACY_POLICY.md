# Privacy Policy

## Definition

In this agreement, "we" defined as the maintainers and contributors of Ciyue.

## Our Data Practices

Ciyue is an open-source software. We do not provide any services that collect, store, or process your personal data through Ciyue itself.

## Ciyue's Functionality

Ciyue operates as software that can integrate with third-party services (e.g., AI providers) and user-selected storage servers. Ciyue does not connect to those services by default; it only does so when users configure and invoke the relevant features. If you use cloud sync, the selected wordbook, tags, flashcards, review history, and any dictionaries you explicitly select are transferred to your configured WebDAV, SFTP, or S3-compatible storage. Ciyue maintainers do not operate or receive this sync data. Passwords, SFTP private keys and passphrases, and S3 secret access keys are stored in the device's secure storage; non-secret connection settings are kept in local preferences.

## Third-Party Policies

By using any third-party services Ciyue connects to, you agree to the respective privacy policies and terms of service of those providers. We encourage you to review these policies to understand how your data may be handled.

## Disclaimer

We are not responsible for the data practices of these third-party services.
