# Privacy Policy

## Definition

In this agreement, "we" defined as the maintainers and contributors of Ciyue.

## Our Data Practices

Ciyue is an open-source software. We do not provide any services that collect, store, or process your personal data through Ciyue itself.

## Ciyue's Functionality

Ciyue operates as software that integrates with third-party services (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini) to deliver its functionality. Ciyue does not connect to these services by default; it only does so when users configure certain options.

Cloud sync is optional. After a user connects a WebDAV, Google Drive, or OneDrive account and confirms a sync, Ciyue transfers wordbook entries and tags, flashcard scheduling data, review history, and sync metadata to that user's selected storage. Dictionary packages are transferred only when individually selected. OAuth tokens and WebDAV passwords are kept in the device's operating-system secure storage. Ciyue does not operate a cloud-sync backend or receive a copy of this data. Disconnecting removes the local connection credentials and profile; it does not delete files already stored with the provider.

## Third-Party Policies

By using any third-party services Ciyue connects to, you agree to the respective privacy policies and terms of service of those providers. We encourage you to review these policies to understand how your data may be handled.

## Disclaimer

We are not responsible for the data practices of these third-party services.
