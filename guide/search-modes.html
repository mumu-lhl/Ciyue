Search modes ​

The search bar can look up words in four different ways. Tap the mode button — it sits at the right edge of the search bar, and also in the expanded search view next to the clear (✕) button — to cycle through them. The current mode is shown by its icon, and on long-press by a tooltip such as "Search mode: Prefix". Your choice is saved and restored the next time you open Ciyue.

The four modes, in cycle order:

ModeIconMatchesNeeds the full-text index?
Prefix (default)⏮Headwords starting with your query.No
Suffix⏭Headwords ending with your query.Yes
Multi-word🔗Headwords containing every term you type, not necessarily adjacent.Yes
Full text🔍Text inside definitions, not just headwords.Yes

Prefix — the default ​

Headwords that begin with what you type, e.g. app → apple, application. This is the original Ciyue behaviour and needs no setup: it works as soon as a dictionary is loaded.

Suffix — a reverse dictionary ​

Headwords that end with your query, so you can look a word up by its tail. This is the reverse-dictionary / 倒序词典 idea: if you can only remember the ending — say you want a word ending in "动" (movement) but can't recall 翕动 — typing 动 lists 按兵不动, 摆动, 半自动, 暴动, 被动 … and you pick the one you meant.

Suffix matching is served by a reversed-headword column added to the full-text index, so it needs that index (see below).

Multi-word — combine terms ​

Headwords that contain all the characters you type, in any order and not necessarily next to each other. Search 中国 (or 中 国) and the suggestions include 中国 (adjacent, in order), 国中 (adjacent, reversed), 中国通 and 大秦景教流行中国碑 (both containing the adjacent pair 中国), and 中立国, 中华民国鄂州约法, 中华民国北伐铁血团 (with other characters in between). Every headword holds both characters, but they need not be adjacent or in order — an intent neither prefix nor suffix matching can express.

Full text — search the definitions ​

Matches your query inside the definitions, not just the headwords. Type a concept or a phrase and Ciyue lists every entry whose article mentions it, with a snippet of the surrounding text. This is the same engine as the standalone "Search in definitions" results, now available directly in the search bar.

The full-text index ​

Suffix, Multi-word and Full text all read from the full-text index that Ciyue builds per dictionary. Prefix does not use it.

When you first use one of those three modes, Ciyue starts building the index in the background — the same build the full-text page runs. Until an index is ready, the search bar shows a status row instead of results:

  • Building… — the index is being created; results appear once it finishes.
  • Index not built — tap it to open the full-text page, where the build runs and reports progress.
  • Index failed — tap it to retry from the full-text page.

You can also build and manage every dictionary's index at once from Settings → Manage Dictionaries → the index-overview icon, which lists each dictionary's status, on-disk size and build / cancel / rebuild actions.
