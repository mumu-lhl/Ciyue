Guide ​

Welcome to the Ciyue documentation. Start here to install the app, add dictionaries, and get the most out of it.

Contents ​

  • Getting Started — install Ciyue on your device, download dictionaries, and set up the basics.
  • Translation — translate with 20+ free, official-API, and AI sources in parallel and compare the results.
  • Cloud Sync — keep your wordbook, tags, flashcards, and history in sync across devices via WebDAV, SFTP, or S3.
  • Word-Form Lookup — resolve inflected forms back to their lemma with Hunspell, and choose between fallback and supplement modes.
  • Appearance — make dictionary pages follow the app theme, fix entries that are unreadable in dark mode, and inject custom CSS.
  • Pronunciation — which audio formats play, and how to convert a pack the device cannot decode.
  • FAQ — answers to common questions.
